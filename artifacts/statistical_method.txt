Full-sample descriptive logistic model, n=7,043 complete records (0 omitted for missing model fields). Predictors were prespecified: tenure per 12 months, monthly charges per 10 source-currency units, contract, internet service and senior-citizen status. Reference categories: Contract: Month-to-month; InternetService: DSL; SeniorCitizen: No. TotalCharges is excluded to limit collinearity. Odds ratios above 1 indicate higher adjusted churn odds; below 1 indicate lower adjusted odds. These are adjusted associations, not causal effects, risk ratios or predictions from the held-out ML model. 95% Wald confidence intervals and unadjusted coefficient p-values assume independent observations, a correctly specified logit, linear numeric effects on log-odds, and no important omitted confounding.
