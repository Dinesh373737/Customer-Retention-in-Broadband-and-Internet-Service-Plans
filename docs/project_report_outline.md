# Project report outline

1. **Title and abstract:** Customer Retention in Broadband and Internet Service Plans; academic scope and decision-support objective.
2. **Problem and motivation:** Subscription churn, customer relationship management, opportunity cost and campaign resource allocation. Separate prediction from causal intervention effects.
3. **Literature and sources:** Cite the IBM dataset repository, Scikit-learn methodology documentation, XGBoost documentation and the original SHAP research where used. Discuss the limits of sample datasets and observational associations.
4. **Dataset and quality:** Source, actual schema and counts, class balance, missing values, duplicates, normalization and a data dictionary. Attach `artifacts/data_quality.json`.
5. **Customer journey:** Explain all seven stages in `docs/customer_journey.md`; identify historical, predicted and conceptual stages.
6. **Method:** Training-only preprocessing, stratified independent partitions, tuning grids, calibration, the actual weighted probability ensemble, selected risk policy and leakage prevention. Attach generated training metadata.
7. **Exploration and statistical findings:** Group rates with sample sizes and uncertainty, descriptive statistics, association tests, effect sizes, adjusted p-values and logistic odds ratios with confidence intervals. Do not infer causality.
8. **Predictive results:** Insert generated held-out comparison, ROC/PR curves, confusion matrices and calibration assessment. State the validation-selected preferred model and report whether the hybrid improved test metrics without retroactively choosing weights.
9. **Decision support:** Show anonymized customer examples, explicitly labelled explanations, transparent evidence-linked suggestions and limitations. Full-data customer scoring is not held-out evaluation.
10. **Economics:** Define contribution margin, campaign period and currency. Present editable hypothetical incremental success assumptions, net benefit, break-even and sensitivity analysis. Discuss pilot design, opportunity cost and budget allocation.
11. **Limitations:** Historical sample, absent network/complaint/offer outcomes, possible sampling bias, fairness concerns, associative explanations, probability uncertainty and uncertain transfer to other providers.
12. **Future work:** Controlled intervention pilot, uplift estimates, temporal validation, outcome monitoring and governance. Clearly distinguish future proposals from implemented features.
13. **Conclusion and reproducibility:** Discuss actual results and practical limits; provide launch/test instructions, package versions, seed and saved-artifact fingerprint.

Never insert predicted model scores, customer responses or financial results until generated by the relevant real analysis or explicitly labelled scenario.
